Commercial Procurement Guide

Frequently Asked Wholesale Questions

Essential operational and phytosanitary answers for global import compliance officers.

help

What is HerboJet's Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

Our standard minimum order quantity is 500 Kilograms per individual botanical cut for consolidated LCL air or ocean cargo. For full ocean container dispatches, we support mixed FCL loads (up to 4–6 botanical items within a single 20ft container, totaling 6 to 9 MT depending on bulk density).

tune

Can HerboJet calibrate custom sifting & particle sizes?

Yes. Our processing plant operates precision triple-deck vibrating sieves and air separators. We manufacture cuts spanning from 0.5mm tea-bag cut (TBC) up to 1–4mm C&S, whole flower heads, or 80-mesh fine botanical powders with specified bulk density parameters.

description

What export documents accompany every oceanic consignment?

Every shipment includes an original Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Original Clean on Board Ocean Bill of Lading, Official Egyptian Phytosanitary Certificate, Certificate of Origin (Eur.1 / Form A where applicable), Fumigation Certificate, and batch Certificate of Analysis (CoA).

payments

What international payment terms are accepted?

For initial contracts, we operate on 30% advance telegraphic transfer (T/T) with 70% against faxed shipping documents, or 100% Irrevocable Letter of Credit (L/C at Sight) issued by a prime international commercial bank. Open accounts are available for audited annual contract clients.

inventory

How are pre-shipment evaluation samples dispatched?

We dispatch 200g–500g vacuum-sealed samples directly from our active lot stocks via DHL Express or FedEx International Priority. Samples typically arrive at European, North American, or Asian laboratory facilities within 3 to 4 business days.

format_image_left

Is steam sterilization available for microbiological control?

Yes. Upon buyer request, our botanicals can undergo gentle continuous saturated steam sterilization to reduce Total Plate Counts (TPC) to < 10⁴ CFU/g and guarantee negative Salmonella/E. Coli, while preserving volatile essential oil profiles and color integrity.
