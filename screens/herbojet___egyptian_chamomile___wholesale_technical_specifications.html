Seed-to-Shipment Direct Sourcing

Nile Valley Agricultural Heritage & Shade Drying

The rich alluvial silt soils and arid, temperate winter microclimate of Upper Egypt’s Nile Basin provide the absolute premier global terroir for matricaria chamomilla cultivation. HerboJet maintains exclusive cultivation contracts over 1,400+ feddans across Fayoum, Beni Suef, and Menia.

1
Planting Cycle: October – November

Sown using certified non-GMO seed stock adapted for maximum chamazulene and alpha-bisabolol accumulation.

2
Multi-Flush Hand Harvesting: January – April

Flowers are selectively hand-picked at peak bloom disc aperture across 4 to 6 continuous seasonal pickings.

3
UV-Shielded Shaded Tunnel Drying

Dried exclusively under covered, ventilated drying structures away from direct sun exposure, completely eliminating photolytic degradation of essential oil and floret pigmentation.
