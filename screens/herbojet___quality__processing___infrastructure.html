Analytical Rigor

Standard Testing Protocols & Parameters

Every batch exported by HerboJet complies with international food safety thresholds, European Pharmacopoeia (Ph. Eur.) monographs, and the American Herbal Products Association (AHPA) guidelines.

verified_user Batch Releases Confirmed via 100% C.o.A. Attachment
Physical Parameter Target: < 8.0% - 10.0%

Moisture Content & Water Activity

Evaluated using automated halogen infrared moisture analyzers and Karl Fischer volumetric titration for sensitive seed oils to prevent microbial activity in transit.

Standard Test Reference ISO 939 / European Pharmacopoeia (Ph. Eur. 2.2.32)
Active Phytochemistry Target: Monograph Max

Volatile Essential Oil Assay

Clevenger hydro-distillation apparatus for 4 continuous hours. Quantifies volatile terpene yield (e.g., Peppermint > 1.2% v/w; Chamomile > 0.4% v/w; Basil > 0.5% v/w).

Standard Test Reference ISO 6571 / Ph. Eur. Method 2.8.12
Physical Purity Foreign Matter < 0.5%

Foreign Matter & Botanical Purity

Multi-deck laboratory sieves and stereomicroscopic examination. Guarantees complete absence of glass, metal fragments, animal excreta, and non-target botanical species.

Standard Test Reference ISO 927 / ASTA Method 14.0 Physical Cleanliness
Bioburden Panel Pathogens: Negative / 25g

Microbiological Screen

Comprehensive bacteriological profiling: Aerobic Mesophilic Plate Count (TPC), Yeasts & Molds, Bile-tolerant Gram-negative bacteria, E. coli, and absolute Salmonella absence in 25g/375g.

Standard Test Reference ISO 4833-1 / FDA BAM / Ph. Eur. 5.1.8 Cat. B
Heavy Metals & Toxins EU Reg 2023/915 Caps

Chemical & Heavy Metals Assay

Inductively Coupled Plasma Mass Spectrometry (ICP-MS) detection for Lead (Pb), Cadmium (Cd), Arsenic (As), and Mercury (Hg), alongside HPLC testing for Aflatoxins (B1, B2, G1, G2) and Ochratoxin A.

Standard Test Reference EPA 6020B / EN 15763 / ICP-MS Methodology
Pesticide Residues 500+ Active Compounds

Pesticide Multi-Residue Screen

High-sensitivity GC-MS/MS and LC-MS/MS multi-residue pesticide screens conducted via Eurofins/SGS to ensure total compliance with Regulation (EC) No 396/2005 Maximum Residue Limits (MRLs).

Standard Test Reference DIN EN 15662 (QuEChERS Method)
